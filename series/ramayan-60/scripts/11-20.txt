## 11 | The Coronation Plan | Tomorrow, a king would be crowned…
src: Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 1-5
[Ayodhya at peace, happy citizens] For twelve years, Ayodhya was happy under Dasharatha. Rama and Sita lived in joy, and the people adored their eldest prince.
[Dasharatha in council] He called his ministers and the kings of the land. 'I have served long enough,' he said. 'I wish to crown Rama as Yuvaraja, the heir to the throne.'
[the court cheering] The whole court roared its approval. 'Rama is truthful, brave and kind,' they said. 'Crown him!'
[Vasishtha announcing the auspicious day] Vasishtha fixed the ceremony for the very next morning, under the star Pushya. Bharata and Shatrughna were away at their uncle's kingdom.
[city decorating, flowers and lamps] News spread, and the city burst into celebration. Streets were sprinkled with water, flags flew from every roof, and lamps were lit through the night.
[Rama's calm, humble face] Only Rama stayed quiet and humble. He never wanted the throne. He wanted only to serve.

## 12 | Manthara's Poison | One whisper changed everything.
src: Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 7-9
[Manthara on the palace roof watching the celebration] From a palace terrace, a hunchbacked maid named Manthara watched the flags and flowers with a sour face.
[Manthara asking why the city celebrates] 'Why is the city rejoicing?' she asked. When she heard Rama would be crowned, she burned with anger and ran to her queen.
[Manthara whispering to Kaikeyi] 'Wake up, my queen! Rama becomes king tomorrow, and your son Bharata will have nothing.'
[Kaikeyi giving Manthara a jewel in joy] But Kaikeyi was delighted. She gave Manthara a jewel. 'Rama is like my own son,' she said.
[Manthara throwing the jewel down] Manthara threw it away. 'Remember the two boons the king once gave you, when you saved his life in the war against the demons?'
[Kaikeyi's smile fading] At first Kaikeyi loved Rama as her own son. But slowly, the poison of doubt took hold.
[Kaikeyi removing her jewels, lying on the floor] She tore off her jewels, put on soiled clothes, and lay on the bare floor of the anger chamber.

## 13 | Kaikeyi's Boons | Dharma or heartbreak?
src: Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 10-14
[Dasharatha entering the dark chamber] Dasharatha came looking for his favorite queen, and found her on the floor of the dark chamber, her hair loose and her jewels scattered.
[the king kneeling beside her] 'Who has hurt you?' he cried. 'Ask anything, and I swear by Rama, whom I love more than my life, I will give it.'
[Kaikeyi's hard face] Kaikeyi rose. 'Two boons you promised me long ago. First: Bharata becomes king. Second: Rama goes to the forest for fourteen years, dressed as a hermit.'
[Dasharatha collapsing] The king fell to the ground as if struck by lightning. He begged, wept and pleaded. He even touched her feet.
[Kaikeyi turning away] 'You are the king of the Ikshvaku line,' she said coldly. 'Will you break your word?' Kaikeyi did not move.
[night passing, the king awake in grief] The whole night passed. The king lay awake, calling Rama's name, praying that the dawn would never come.
[Dasharatha's face in tears] A king bound by his word. A father bound by love.

## 14 | Rama Accepts Exile | The kingdom lost. The dharma kept.
src: Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 16-19, 37
[Rama arriving at the palace at dawn] At dawn, Rama came to greet his father, expecting a blessing for his coronation. He found him broken, unable even to speak.
[Rama turning to Kaikeyi] 'What has happened, mother?' asked Rama. 'Have I displeased my father?'
[Kaikeyi telling him the news] Kaikeyi told him everything: the two boons, the throne for Bharata, the forest for him.
[Rama smiling gently] Rama did not argue or frown. 'If my father's word needs it, I go gladly,' he said. 'Why would I need the crown when I have his honor?'
[Kaushalya weeping] He went to his mother Kaushalya. She fainted with grief, and begged to come with him.
[Lakshmana in fury, hand on his sword] Lakshmana raged and wanted to fight for the throne. Rama calmed him. 'This is destiny,' he said. 'No one is to blame.'
[Rama removing his royal robes, putting on bark cloth] Rama took off his silk and jewels and put on clothes of tree bark.
[Rama walking away from the throne] He gave up a kingdom as easily as one lets go of a flower.

## 15 | Sita and Lakshmana Follow | Three hearts, one path.
src: Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 26-31, 40
[Rama telling Sita to stay] Rama asked Sita to stay behind. 'The forest is hard and dangerous. There are snakes, wild beasts, thorns and demons.'
[Sita holding his hand] 'Where you are is my home,' she said. 'Even a palace without you is a jungle. With you, the forest will be a garden.'
[Sita in tears, determined] When Rama still refused, she wept and declared she would die rather than be left behind. At last, Rama agreed.
[Lakshmana touching Rama's feet] Lakshmana too refused to be left behind. 'I will walk ahead of you with my bow,' he said. 'I will serve you day and night.'
[Sumitra blessing Lakshmana] His mother Sumitra blessed him: 'Think of Rama as your father, Sita as your mother, and the forest as Ayodhya.'
[three figures leaving the palace gate] The three left Ayodhya in bark cloth, in the chariot of the minister Sumantra.
[the whole city following in tears] The whole city followed them in tears, running behind the chariot.
[old Dasharatha falling in the dust] Behind them, the old king fell in the dust, watching until the chariot was gone.

## 16 | Guha and the Ganga | The forest begins.
src: Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 46-52 (Kevat washing Rama's feet is from the Ramcharitmanas)
[Rama leaving the sleeping citizens behind at night] The people of Ayodhya would not turn back, so Rama left quietly at night while they slept.
[a wide river at dawn, a boatman waiting] At the Ganga, the boatman king Guha of Shringaverapura welcomed Rama as a dear friend.
[Guha offering food and his kingdom] He brought food and offered his whole kingdom. Rama refused gently. He had a vow to keep: no royal comforts for fourteen years.
[Guha washing Rama's feet] Later tellings say the boatman washed Rama's feet before letting him in the boat, in case his touch turned the boat into a woman, as it had Ahalya.
[Rama sending Sumantra home] Rama sent the minister Sumantra home with the empty chariot and a message of love for his father.
[the boat crossing the river] Guha rowed them across. Sita folded her hands and prayed to the Ganga for their safe return.
[Rama cutting his hair into a sage's matted locks] On the far bank, Rama and Lakshmana matted their hair with the sap of a banyan tree.
[Rama dressed as a forest sage] Here Rama gave up the last sign of royalty and became a forest sage.

## 17 | Dasharatha's Death | A father who died of love.
src: Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 57-64
[Ayodhya, silent and dark] With Rama gone, Ayodhya lost its light. No fires were lit, no songs were sung, and the markets were empty.
[Sumantra returning with the empty chariot] When Sumantra returned with the empty chariot, the king fainted.
[Dasharatha on his bed, weeping] Dasharatha cried Rama's name day and night. Then he remembered a curse from long ago.
[young Dasharatha hunting by a river at night] As a young prince, he had shot an arrow at a sound in the dark, thinking it was an elephant drinking.
[the dying hermit boy with a water pot] It was a young hermit filling a pot of water for his blind parents. Later tellings call him Shravan Kumar.
[the old blind parents of the hermit] The grieving parents cursed him: 'You too will die of grief, separated from your son.'
[the king whispering in his last breath] Now the curse had come true. On the sixth night, at midnight, he whispered, 'Rama, Rama,' and was gone.
[Kaushalya finding him at dawn] At dawn, the queens found him still. The whole palace broke into cries.

## 18 | Bharata Returns | A brother's true love.
src: Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 68-79
[Bharata dreaming of bad omens] Far away in his uncle's kingdom, Bharata had a terrible dream about his father.
[Bharata riding home from his uncle's kingdom] Bharata was away when all this happened. Messengers called him home in haste, without telling him why.
[empty streets of Ayodhya] He felt something was wrong the moment he saw the silent city. No one greeted him. No one smiled.
[Kaikeyi telling him] Kaikeyi proudly told him: 'Your father is gone. Rama is in exile. The throne is yours.'
[Bharata, furious and heartbroken] Bharata was shattered. 'You have destroyed my family for my sake? You are no mother of mine! I want nothing of this.'
[Bharata falling at Kaushalya's feet] He ran to Kaushalya, fell at her feet and swore he had known nothing of the plot.
[Bharata performing his father's funeral] He performed his father's last rites with Shatrughna, weeping beside the funeral fire.
[Bharata rejecting the crown] When the ministers offered him the crown, he refused it. He set out to bring his brother home.

## 19 | Meeting at Chitrakoot | Each gave, neither took.
src: Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 83-112
[Bharata and the whole city marching into the forest] Bharata led an army of citizens, queens and sages into the forest to find Rama.
[Lakshmana seeing the army from a tree] Seeing the army, Lakshmana thought Bharata had come to attack, and raised his bow. Rama stopped him. 'Bharata would never harm us.'
[Rama's small hut at Chitrakoot] At Chitrakoot, Bharata found Rama's small hut. He fell at Rama's feet, weeping. 'Return and rule, brother.'
[Rama learning of his father's death] Then Rama learned that his father was dead. He wept, and offered water to his father's spirit at the river.
[Rama gently lifting him] Rama embraced Bharata. 'Our father gave his word. I must keep it, and you must rule.'
[Bharata holding firm] Bharata would not give in. He sat outside the hut and vowed to fast until Rama agreed.
[the sages watching the two brothers] For hours, two brothers each tried to give the kingdom to the other. The sages watching had never seen such a contest.
[the two brothers sitting face to face] Neither would take the throne. Each only wanted the other to have it.

## 20 | The Paduka | The throne belonged to Rama.
src: Valmiki, Ayodhya Kanda 112-115
[Bharata asking for Rama's sandals] At last, Bharata asked for one thing. 'Give me your sandals.'
[Rama placing his sandals in Bharata's hands] Rama agreed. He stepped onto a pair of golden sandals and gave them to his brother.
[Bharata carrying the sandals on his head] Bharata carried them on his head all the way home, as if he were carrying Rama himself.
[Bharata's promise to Rama] 'If you do not return on the very day the fourteen years end,' he said, 'I will enter the fire.'
[the sandals on the throne of Ayodhya] He placed them on the throne and ruled in Rama's name, as a servant. Every decision was first laid before the sandals.
[Bharata living in a simple hut at Nandigram] He would not live in the palace. He lived in a simple hut at Nandigram, outside the city, wearing bark cloth and matted hair.
[Bharata at dawn praying] He lived like an ascetic for fourteen years, waiting for his brother.
[Rama leaving Chitrakoot for the deeper forest] And Rama, so that the people would stop coming, went deeper into the forest.
