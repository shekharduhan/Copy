## 41 | Lankini | Lanka's doom had begun.
src: Valmiki, Sundara Kanda 2-3
[golden Lanka glowing on the horizon] At last Hanuman landed on Mount Lamba in Lanka, a city of gold with towering walls, built on a mountain peak in the middle of the sea.
[Lanka's walls, moats and guards] Its walls were gold, its gates were guarded by thousands of demons with spears and bows, and its moats were full of crocodiles.
[Hanuman shrinking to the size of a cat] So he waited for night. Then he shrank to the size of a cat and crept toward the gate in the moonlight.
[Lankini, the guardian demoness, blocking him] Lankini, the guardian spirit of the city, stopped him. She was huge and terrible, with blazing eyes. 'No one enters Lanka,' she roared, 'without my permission.'
[Hanuman striking her once with his left hand] Hanuman did not want to hurt her badly. He tapped her lightly, just once, with his left hand.
[Lankini falling, remembering a prophecy] She fell, and remembered Brahma's old prophecy: 'When a monkey defeats you, Lanka's end is near.'
[Lankini letting Hanuman pass] 'Go in, mighty one,' she said, 'and find the daughter of Janaka.'

## 42 | Ashok Vatika | Sita, found at last.
src: Valmiki, Sundara Kanda 4-15
[Hanuman searching Ravana's palace at night] Hanuman searched Ravana's palace room by room, past drunken guards, dancing halls and golden chambers, but found no Sita.
[Hanuman seeing Ravana asleep among his queens] He saw Ravana asleep, surrounded by beautiful queens. For a moment he thought one was Sita, then shook his head. 'Sita would never sleep here, happy.'
[Hanuman sitting in despair on a wall] He searched every house in Lanka. Nothing. He sat on a wall in despair. Had she died? Had he failed Rama?
[Hanuman gazing at a garden of Ashoka trees] At last he saw a garden of Ashoka trees, glowing in the moonlight.
[a thin, pale woman in plain clothes under a tree] Beneath one tree sat a thin, pale woman in plain, dusty yellow clothes, her hair in a single braid, her eyes full of tears.
[Sita surrounded by demonesses] Ferocious demonesses guarded her, with the faces of horses, boars and buffaloes. She wept, whispering Rama's name.
[Hanuman hiding in the tree above] Hanuman hid in the branches above, his heart pounding. He had found her.

## 43 | Hanuman Meets Sita | A voice from the branches.
src: Valmiki, Sundara Kanda 18-31
[Ravana arriving in the garden at dawn with his queens] At dawn, Ravana came to the garden, surrounded by his queens and torchbearers, with music playing.
[Ravana threatening Sita in the garden] 'Marry me,' he said, 'and all of Lanka is yours.' When she refused, he roared that she had two months to change her mind.
[Sita, firm and unafraid] Sita placed a blade of grass between them and refused to even look at him. 'Rama will come for me.'
[Ravana leaving in a rage] Ravana stormed away. The demonesses taunted her until she was alone.
[Trijata telling of her dream] Then an old demoness, Trijata, woke up shaking. 'I dreamed that Rama came on a white elephant, and Lanka sank into the sea. Do not harm her.'
[Hanuman softly singing Rama's story from the tree] Then a gentle voice from the tree began to sing the story of Rama: Ayodhya, the exile, the golden deer, the search.
[Sita looking up in wonder] Sita looked up and saw a small monkey among the leaves, his hands folded.

## 44 | The Ring and the Jewel | A message. A jewel. A hope.
src: Valmiki, Sundara Kanda 32-40
[Sita afraid it is a trick] At first, Sita was afraid. Was this Ravana in disguise again?
[Hanuman dropping Rama's ring into Sita's hands] Hanuman climbed down, bowed, and dropped Rama's ring into her lap. She saw Rama's name on it and wept with joy, as if she had seen Rama himself.
[Hanuman describing Rama's grief] He told her how Rama could not sleep or eat, and spoke her name every night.
[Hanuman offering to carry her away] He offered to carry her back right then, on his back, across the ocean.
[Sita refusing, explaining] 'No,' said Sita. 'Rama must come and rescue me himself, for his honor. And I will not willingly touch another man.'
[Sita telling the story of the crow] She told him a secret only Rama knew: how Rama once punished a crow that hurt her with a single blade of grass, made into a divine weapon.
[Sita giving her jewel, the Choodamani] She gave him her hair jewel, the Choodamani, as proof. 'Tell Rama I am waiting. I can live one month more.'

## 45 | Lanka Burns | Lanka in flames.
src: Valmiki, Sundara Kanda 41-54
[Hanuman destroying the garden] Before leaving, Hanuman wanted to test Ravana's strength. He tore through the palace garden, uprooting trees and smashing pavilions.
[Hanuman fighting the guards and Akshayakumara] Guards came and fell. Eighty thousand of them. Then Ravana's own young son Akshayakumara fell too.
[Indrajit's arrow, Hanuman bound] Ravana sent his greatest son, Indrajit. Hanuman allowed Indrajit's Brahmastra to bind him, so he could meet Ravana.
[Hanuman captured, brought before Ravana] He was dragged before Ravana. Hanuman made a seat of his own tail, coiled higher than the king's throne.
[Hanuman warning Ravana] He warned him: 'Return Sita, or face Rama. Not even the gods can save you from his arrows.'
[Ravana ordering death, Vibhishana stopping him] Ravana ordered his death, but his brother Vibhishana stopped him. 'A messenger must not be killed.'
[a burning tail, Hanuman leaping over rooftops] So Ravana ordered his tail set on fire. Hanuman shrank, broke free, and leapt from roof to roof.
[Lanka burning to the ground] He burned Lanka to the ground, sparing only the Ashoka grove where Sita sat. Then he dipped his tail in the sea.

## 46 | The Report to Rama | Hope returned.
src: Valmiki, Sundara Kanda 56-68
[Hanuman flying back over the ocean] Hanuman flew back over the ocean and landed on Mahendra mountain with a roar, where the monkeys cheered his return.
[the monkeys celebrating in Madhuvana] On the way home they celebrated in Sugriva's honey garden, Madhuvana, eating and drinking until the guards complained.
[Sugriva smiling] When Sugriva heard, he smiled. 'They would only dare to do this if they had found Sita.'
[Hanuman kneeling before Rama] Hanuman knelt before Rama and told him everything. 'I have seen Sita. She is alive and faithful.'
[Hanuman giving the Choodamani jewel] He placed her jewel in Rama's hands.
[Rama holding the jewel to his heart] Rama held it to his heart and wept. He remembered the day Janaka gave it to Sita at their wedding.
[Rama embracing Hanuman] 'I have nothing to give you, Hanuman, but this embrace,' said Rama, and held him close.
[Rama rising, ready for war] Then Rama rose. 'We march to the ocean. Now.'

## 47 | March to the Ocean | Even Rama's patience has limits.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 4-5, 21
[millions of monkeys and bears marching] Sugriva's army set out for the southern shore, millions strong. Monkeys and bears covered the mountains like a moving forest.
[Hanuman carrying Rama, Angada carrying Lakshmana] Hanuman carried Rama on his shoulders, and Angada carried Lakshmana.
[the army eating fruit, crossing rivers] They ate fruit and honey from the forests, crossed rivers and mountains, and never stopped to rest.
[the vast ocean before them] They reached the sea and stopped. The waves were endless. How could an army cross such water?
[Rama looking across the sea toward Lanka] Rama stood on the shore, looking south toward Lanka, where Sita waited.
[Rama sitting in prayer on the shore] Rama lay on a bed of sacred grass on the shore and prayed to Samudra, the ocean god, for three days and nights.
[the ocean silent and still] The sea did not answer. The waves rolled on as if Rama did not exist.
[Rama's eyes turning red with anger] On the fourth morning, Rama's eyes turned red. He reached for his bow.

## 48 | Vibhishana Joins Rama | Refuge for all who ask.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 9-19
[Vibhishana advising Ravana in court] In Lanka, Ravana's younger brother Vibhishana begged him: 'Return Sita and end this war. Bad omens are everywhere.'
[the ministers mocking Vibhishana] The ministers laughed. Indrajit called him a coward.
[Ravana kicking him from the throne] Ravana kicked him out of the court in a rage. 'You are an enemy dressed as a brother.'
[Vibhishana crossing the sky to Rama's camp] Vibhishana flew across the sea with four loyal ministers to Rama's camp and asked for refuge.
[the monkeys suspicious, Sugriva warning] Many advisors warned it was a trick. 'He is Ravana's brother,' Sugriva said. 'Do not trust him.'
[Hanuman speaking for Vibhishana] Only Hanuman spoke for him. 'I have seen his heart. He is good.'
[Rama welcoming him] But Rama said, 'I never turn away someone who seeks shelter. Even if Ravana himself came to me, I would protect him.'
[Rama crowning Vibhishana with seawater] He crowned Vibhishana king of Lanka that day, with water from the sea.

## 49 | Ram Setu | A bridge built by faith.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 21-22 (the stones marked 'Ram' and the squirrel come from later retellings)
[Rama raising his bow at the ocean] When Samudra still did not appear, Rama raised his bow to dry up the sea. The waves boiled and sea creatures fled.
[Samudra appearing, folding his hands] The ocean god rose from the waves with folded hands and begged for mercy. 'Build a bridge,' he advised. 'I will hold it.'
[Nala and Neel directing the work] He told Rama that the monkey Nala, son of the divine architect Vishwakarma, could build it. Nala and Neel guided the work.
[monkeys uprooting trees and mountains] Monkeys uprooted trees and broke off whole mountain peaks and threw them into the sea.
[monkeys carrying boulders marked 'Ram'] Later tellings say each stone was marked with Rama's name, and so it floated.
[a small squirrel helping, Rama stroking it] Even a little squirrel rolled in the sand and shook it onto the bridge. Rama stroked its back, and the three lines on squirrels, they say, are his fingers.
[the army crossing the finished bridge] In five days, the bridge to Lanka was ready, and the army crossed.

## 50 | Angada's Mission | War is declared.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 41 (the planted foot comes from later retellings)
[Rama's army camped at Lanka's walls] Rama's army camped at the gates of Lanka, and Rama looked up at the golden city where Sita was held.
[Rama giving Angada a mission] Before war, Rama gave Ravana one last chance. He sent Prince Angada, Vali's son, as his messenger.
[Angada entering Ravana's court] Angada walked into the demon king's court without fear, and stood before the ten-headed king.
[Ravana mocking Angada] Ravana mocked him. 'Your father was my friend. Why serve the man who killed him?'
[Angada planting his foot firmly] 'Return Sita,' Angada said. When the court laughed, he planted his foot and said, 'If any of you can move it, Rama will go home.'
[warriors failing to lift his foot] One by one, the mightiest demons strained and pulled. No one could move it.
[Ravana rising to try himself] Even Ravana rose to try. Angada stepped back. 'Do not touch my feet. Touch Rama's feet, and you will be saved.'
[Angada leaping out of the palace] Ravana did not listen. The last chance for peace was gone. Angada leapt through the palace roof and flew back to Rama.
