## 51 | The War Begins | The great war begins.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 42-44
[two vast armies facing each other across Lanka's plain] Rama's monkey army stood before Ravana's demon legions. Drums thundered. Conches roared across the plain.
[Rama dividing his army at Lanka's four gates] Rama divided his army to attack all four gates of Lanka at once. He took the north gate himself, where Ravana waited.
[Rama and Lakshmana at the front] Rama and Lakshmana led from the front. On the walls, Ravana watched with his ten heads, his twenty eyes burning.
[demons pouring out of the gates] The gates opened, and demons poured out on elephants and chariots, with swords, maces and spears.
[boulders and trees hurled at the gates] The monkeys charged with rocks, trees and bare hands, roaring Rama's name.
[the battle raging until night] The battle raged all day. Blood ran like rivers. When night fell, the demons grew stronger, and still the monkeys fought on.
[Hanuman and Angada fighting demon generals] Demon generals fell one by one to Hanuman, Angada and Sugriva.
[Indrajit watching from the clouds] But Ravana's son Indrajit was yet to fight. Watching from the clouds, he smiled.

## 52 | Nagapasha | Garuda saves the day.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 44-50
[Indrajit hiding in the clouds, invisible] Indrajit, master of illusion, fought from the clouds where no one could see him. He had once defeated Indra himself, and so got his name.
[serpent arrows binding Rama and Lakshmana] He shot the Nagapasha, arrows of living serpents, which bound both brothers from head to toe.
[the brothers falling] The brothers fell, covered in blood. The monkey army thought they were gone.
[Indrajit announcing victory in Lanka] Indrajit rode back to Lanka and announced that Rama and Lakshmana were dead. Ravana laughed and celebrated.
[Sita shown the fallen brothers from the flying chariot] Ravana even sent Sita in his flying chariot to see them lying on the field. She wept, until Trijata whispered: 'Their faces still shine. They are alive.'
[the monkeys weeping around them] Sugriva and the monkeys wept around the brothers. Vibhishana tried to give them hope.
[Garuda arriving, the serpents fleeing] Then Garuda, the eagle king and enemy of serpents, swept in on a great wind. The snakes fled.
[the brothers rising, healed] He touched their wounds, and they healed. The brothers rose, stronger than before, and the army roared.

## 53 | Lakshmana Falls | A race against the dawn.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 101 (popular tellings give the spear to Indrajit)
[Indrajit leaping into the fray] The fighting grew fiercer each day. Indrajit struck again and again, appearing and vanishing in the sky.
[Lakshmana fighting fiercely] Lakshmana fought him arrow for arrow, protecting his brother's army.
[Indrajit hurling the Shakti spear] At last a deadly Shakti spear was thrown at Lakshmana, blazing like lightning. Valmiki says Ravana threw it; many tellings say Indrajit.
[Lakshmana collapsing] Lakshmana fell, unconscious and barely breathing. The spear stuck deep in his chest.
[Rama cradling his brother] Rama held him and wept. 'I can find another wife. I cannot find another brother.'
[the monkey army falling silent] The whole army fell silent. Without Lakshmana, Rama said, he would not fight on.
[the physician Sushena speaking] The physician Sushena checked his pulse. 'He is alive. There is one hope: the Sanjeevani herb, from a mountain in the Himalayas.'
[Sushena looking at the sky] 'But it must reach him before sunrise.' Every eye turned to Hanuman.

## 54 | Sanjeevani | A brother brought back from the edge.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 74, 101
[Hanuman flying north over mountains] Sanjeevani grew on a faraway mountain in the Himalayas. Hanuman flew there in a single night, faster than the wind.
[the Himalayas under moonlight] He crossed rivers, forests and kingdoms, until the white peaks of the Himalayas shone below him.
[many glowing herbs on the peak] But the mountain glowed with countless herbs, and he could not tell which was the right one. The herbs hid themselves from him.
[Hanuman lifting the entire mountain] So Hanuman lifted the whole mountain and carried it across the sky.
[Hanuman carrying the mountain over the sea, moon behind] He flew back over India and the sea, holding the glowing mountain in his hand like a lamp.
[Lakshmana awakening as Sushena applies the herb] Sushena found the herb, crushed it, and held it to Lakshmana's nose. Lakshmana opened his eyes just before dawn.
[Rama embracing Lakshmana] Rama embraced him, weeping with joy. The monkey army shouted until Lanka shook.
[Hanuman returning the mountain] Then Hanuman carried the mountain back to where it belonged, before the sun rose.

## 55 | Kumbhakarna | A giant who stayed loyal.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 60-67
[Ravana ordering his sleeping giant brother woken] Losing the war, Ravana ordered his sleeping giant brother woken.
[Kumbhakarna asleep in a vast cave-palace] Ravana's brother Kumbhakarna slept for six months at a time, by a boon gone wrong. He had meant to ask Brahma for Indra's throne, but his tongue slipped, and he asked for sleep.
[demons waking him with drums and elephants] The demons woke him with drums, conches and elephants marching over his body. They fed him mountains of food.
[Kumbhakarna advising Ravana] He told Ravana the truth: 'Sita's capture was wrong. Return her.'
[Kumbhakarna marching out anyway] But he still chose to fight for his brother. 'You are my king. I will die for you.'
[the giant crushing the monkey army] He was as tall as a mountain, and monkeys scattered in fear. He grabbed them by the handful and swallowed them. He even carried Sugriva away.
[Rama facing Kumbhakarna] Then Rama faced him. His arrows cut off one arm, then the other, then his legs.
[Rama's arrow striking him down] Rama's last arrow cut off his head. The giant fell into the sea, and the waves rose to the sky. He died with honor.

## 56 | Indrajit | Ravana's pride fell.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 84-91
[Indrajit performing a secret fire ritual] Indrajit slipped away to perform a sacred ritual at the shrine of Nikumbhila. If he finished it, no one could ever defeat him.
[Indrajit's illusion of Sita being killed] To distract Rama, he first created an illusion of Sita and killed her before the monkeys' eyes. Rama fainted with grief.
[Vibhishana explaining the illusion] Vibhishana rushed to him. 'It is a trick! Sita is safe. He is buying time for his ritual.'
[Vibhishana pointing out the hidden place] Vibhishana knew the secret and led Lakshmana to the ritual grounds.
[Lakshmana attacking before the ritual ended] Lakshmana broke the ritual and challenged Indrajit.
[a fierce duel in the sky] They fought for three days and nights. Arrows covered the sky like a storm.
[Lakshmana's final arrow] At last Lakshmana took an arrow and swore: 'If Rama is truly devoted to dharma, let this arrow kill Indrajit.'
[Indrajit falling] After a terrible fight, Lakshmana's arrow found its mark. Ravana's greatest warrior was no more.

## 57 | Ravana Enters the Battle | Only one weapon remained.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 92-108
[Ravana in his golden chariot, eyes burning] Mad with grief for his son, Ravana rode out himself in his golden chariot, his ten heads crowned and his twenty arms armed.
[Ravana scattering the monkey army] He scattered the monkey army like dry leaves in a storm.
[Rama on foot against the demon king's chariot] Rama had no chariot. So Indra sent his own divine chariot with its charioteer, Matali.
[Indra's chariot descending from the sky] It came down from heaven, drawn by green horses, shining like the sun.
[two bows exchanging endless arrows] For days they fought, arrow against arrow. The gods watched from the sky.
[Rama cutting off Ravana's head, another growing] Every time Rama cut off a head, another grew. A hundred heads fell, and a hundred grew back.
[Agastya teaching Rama the Aditya Hridayam] Then the sage Agastya appeared and taught Rama a sacred hymn to the sun, the Aditya Hridayam, to give him strength.
[Rama pausing, then rising with new strength] Rama could not defeat Ravana by ordinary means. Only one weapon remained.

## 58 | Ravana's End | Dharma won.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 108-111 (the nectar in the navel comes from later retellings; Valmiki says the arrow pierced his heart)
[Vibhishana whispering to Rama] Vibhishana whispered the secret: 'Ravana's life is hidden in the nectar in his navel.'
[Matali reminding Rama of the Brahmastra] Matali reminded Rama of the Brahmastra, the weapon of Brahma, given to Rama by Agastya.
[the Brahmastra glowing like fire] Its feathers were the wind, its tip was fire and sun, and its weight was like the mountains Meru and Mandara.
[Rama fitting the glowing arrow] Rama chanted a mantra and released it.
[the arrow striking Ravana's chest] The arrow struck Ravana's navel. In Valmiki's telling, it pierced his heart. The mighty king of Lanka fell.
[the gods showering flowers] The gods showered flowers. The sun shone clear. The wind blew softly again over Lanka.
[Rama honoring the dying king] Even then, Rama asked Vibhishana to give his brother a proper funeral. 'Enmity ends with death,' he said. 'He is as much my brother now as yours.'
[Vibhishana crowned king of Lanka] Vibhishana was crowned king of Lanka, as Rama had promised.

## 59 | Agni Pariksha | Truth passed through fire.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 112-118
[Sita walking out of the Ashok Vatika] After the war, Hanuman brought word of victory to Sita. She was free at last. She could not speak for joy.
[Sita bathed and dressed in a palanquin] She was bathed, dressed in fine clothes and jewels, and brought to Rama in a palanquin.
[Rama seeing Sita, silent and still] When they met, Rama asked her to prove her purity before the world, for the sake of the people's trust.
[Sita in shock, tears falling] Sita was shocked. She had waited for him through a year of terror.
[Sita asking Lakshmana to build a pyre] Sita, hurt but unbroken, asked Lakshmana to build a fire. 'If my heart has never left Rama, may Agni protect me.'
[Sita stepping into the fire] She walked around Rama, folded her hands, and stepped into the fire. Everyone cried out.
[Agni, the fire god, lifting Sita unharmed] Agni himself rose from the flames and returned her to Rama. Not a hair was burnt, not a flower in her garland faded. 'She is pure,' he said.
[Rama taking her hand] Rama took her hand. 'I knew it,' he said. 'But the world had to see it too.'

## 60 | The Return to Ayodhya | Jai Shri Ram. The end.
src: Valmiki, Yuddha Kanda 121-128
[the flying Pushpak Vimana carrying Rama, Sita, Lakshmana] The fourteen years of exile were ending. Rama, Sita and Lakshmana flew home in the Pushpak Vimana, with Hanuman, Sugriva and Vibhishana beside them.
[Rama pointing out places below to Sita] As they flew, Rama pointed down and showed Sita the bridge, Kishkindha, Panchavati, and Chitrakoot, where it had all begun.
[Hanuman flying ahead to Bharata] Rama sent Hanuman ahead, so that Bharata would not enter the fire.
[Bharata waiting with Rama's sandals] Bharata waited at the gates, holding the paduka, exactly as he had for fourteen years.
[the brothers embracing] The brothers embraced, and the sandals were laid at Rama's feet.
[Rama embracing his mothers] Rama bowed to Kaushalya, Sumitra, and even Kaikeyi, with the same love.
[all of Ayodhya lighting diyas] That night, the whole city lit lamps to welcome their king home. We still celebrate that night as Diwali.
[Rama crowned, Sita beside him] Rama was crowned king, and the golden age of Ram Rajya began.
